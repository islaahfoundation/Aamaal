The Power of Gratitude (Ash-Shukr)

“If you are grateful, I will surely increase you [in favor]...” — Surah Ibrahim (14:7)

Gratitude in the believer’s heart is more than a fleeting word on the tongue; it is a lens that transforms ordinary mornings, quiet nights, and even moments of trial into stations of closeness with Allah.

When we pause to notice the blessings already resting in our hands—breath, faith, family, and the ability to remember Him—anxiety loosens its grip and contentment takes root.
