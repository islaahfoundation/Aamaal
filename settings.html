About, support & legal

Screens 99–109
Aamaal v2.6 is a tranquil, ad-free digital sanctuary for daily Dhikr, authentic Duas, and heart-softening Islamic stories. Designed with gentle consistency rather than gamified pressure.
• Home: Track daily goals and launch quick actions.
• Tasbih: Tap anywhere on the bead ring; customize targets (33, 100, 313).
• Duas & Stories: Adjust Arabic script and font scale in Display settings; tap the bookmark ribbon to save to Favourites.
Are my Tasbih counts private? Yes, all counts are stored locally on your device first and only synced if you sign in.
Why did my streak stay gentle? Aamaal celebrates total active days without punishing missed days.
Share Aamaal as Sadaqah Jariyah (ongoing charity) with family and friends.
We never sell personal data, display third-party ads, or track your location without consent. All spiritual progress remains strictly yours and can be exported or erased anytime under the Backup tab.
Duas and Adhkar are verified against Hisnul Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), Sahih al-Bukhari, and Sahih Muslim. Typography powered by open-source Amiri, Cormorant Garamond, and Plus Jakarta Sans licences.
