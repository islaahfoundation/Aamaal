Dua for Travelling

Protection and ease in travel

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ
Subhanalladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wama kunna lahu muqrinin.
Glory be to Him who has subjected this for us, and we could not have done it on our own.
Source: Surah Az-Zukhruf (43:13)
Benefits / Context
  • Seeks Allah's protection during travel.
  • Brings ease and blessings.

Marketplace Supplication

A dua for the marketplace

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadeer.
There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and praise, and He is able to do all things.
Source: Sahih al-Bukhari (7375)
Benefits / Context
  • Brings barakah in trade and earnings.
  • Removes anxiety and difficulties.
